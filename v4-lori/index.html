Meet Emily Chen

Strategy, not just square footage.

In a market full of agents competing on transaction volume, Emily works differently: real estate strategy, executive coaching, and investment thinking, treated as one conversation rather than three separate services. She advises founders and executives — many navigating an equity event, a relocation, or a career pivot — in English and Traditional Chinese, with working experience across the US, Taiwan, China, and Japan.

She holds an Executive MBA from UCLA Anderson and a Strategic Marketing Management certificate from Stanford GSB, and has coached founders and executives independently since 2018 alongside her advisory practice at Compass.
