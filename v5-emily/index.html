Meet Emily Chen

In a real estate market where many focus solely on transaction volume, Emily takes a profoundly different approach. Operating at the intersection of real estate strategy, executive coaching, and investment thinking, she provides a highly personalized advisory service tailored for Silicon Valley's founders and executives.

With an Executive MBA from UCLA Anderson and strategic experience across the US, Taiwan, China, and Japan, Emily advises clients through major life transitions—whether an equity event, relocation, or career pivot—ensuring the optimal outcome for their portfolios and their lives.
