Meet Emily

Quality over quantity.

Many agents compete on transaction volume. Emily competes on judgment. She works at the intersection of real estate strategy, executive coaching, and investment thinking — and treats them as one conversation rather than three separate services.

Advisory, not order-taking.

Her clients are founders and executives, most of them arriving at a moment that is equal parts financial and personal: an equity event, a relocation, a career pivot. The property question is rarely the first question worth asking.
