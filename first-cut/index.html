Most agents tell you what a home is worth. I ask a different question first:

What are you most worried about right now?

Real estate strategy, executive coaching, and investment thinking — for people whose biggest housing decisions arrive tangled up with career decisions. These aren’t separate services. They’re one conversation.

Decision moment · Equity

Your equity just vested. Should any of it become a house?

For senior people at Bay Area tech companies, the hardest real estate question is rarely the house — it’s the concentration. RSUs, a single employer, a single sector, and now possibly a single very large local asset on top of it. Emily works on the diversification question first: what an equity-heavy balance sheet can responsibly carry, and only then which property fits.

Her recent public writing covers the AI-wealth effect on Bay Area housing and RSU-to-real-estate diversification strategy — the market she works in is the one her clients are being paid in.

Decision moment · Relocation

The offer means moving. Should the house decide?

A relocation is a career decision and a housing decision made under the same deadline — and most advice treats them separately. With experience across the US, Taiwan, China, and Japan, Emily has spent her career helping people weigh a role, a market, and a family’s footing as one problem, not three.

Decision moment · Career pivot

The next role changes more than your title.

A promotion, a startup jump, or a step back all change what your finances have to do for you — and what your home has to be. Emily has coached founders and executives as a business strategic coach since 2018, alongside her real estate practice at Compass. The pivot conversation and the property conversation happen at the same table.

About
